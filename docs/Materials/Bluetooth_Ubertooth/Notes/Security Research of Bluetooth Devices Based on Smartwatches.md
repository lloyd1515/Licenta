# Security Research of Bluetooth Devices Based on Smartwatches

> [!abstract] Rezumat / Abstract
> The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices equipped with embedded sensors and software to transmit and exchange data between the physical world and computer systems that collect and process this data. Smartwatches can be considered IoT devices as they are equipped with almost all the necessary technologies; they are wearable computers with built-in sensors and communication systems. Researching Bluetooth security in smartwatches is crucial because the modern world is closely tied to wireless technologies, with Bluetooth being one of the most common. Bluetooth devices store a large amount of personal user information such as geolocation, contacts, messages, and other data. If protection against attacks is insufficient, attackers can gain unauthorized access to users' personal data, leading to severe consequences including identity theft, financial fraud, and other sensitive information leaks. The study describes how potential attackers can exploit Bluetooth technology for data hacking and outlines steps to protect Bluetooth devices from such attacks. Recommendations are provided on configuring Bluetooth devices, using passwords and encryption, and other data security measures. Examples of malicious attacks on Bluetooth devices are illustrated through sniffing attacks using Ubertooth One. This research can be useful for anyone who uses Bluetooth devices, particularly smartwatches, and wants to protect their data from theft.
